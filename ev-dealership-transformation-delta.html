From seven disconnected SaaS to one event-driven platform

A multi-site EV dealership group ran every step on a 3–5-year TOGAF ADM strategy and a 1–3-year Open Agile Architecture plan, shipped in three 20-week waves. This diagram isolates the system-of-record shift: seven point-to-point SaaS tools and a single spreadsheet, replaced by an event-driven platform of which a customer portal sits on top.

Scroll the diagram horizontally to compare both snapshots and the change ledger.

The change is in the spine

The seven SaaS tools don't disappear — they get decoupled behind a new event bus and API gateway (Azure Service Bus + API Management). The spreadsheet that was the single system of record for stock is retired in favour of a central golden-inventory service with an audit trail, and the fragmented customer record is consolidated into a unified CRM (Customer 360) across the five sites and two service centres.

Identity survives, integration doesn't

The systems keep their identities; only the way they talk changes. Further outcomes on the same spine ride on the bus without new point-to-point glue — the customer omnichannel app, Microsoft Fabric KPI dashboards, and predictive lead scoring. Phased go-live 6A → 6B → 6C keeps each wave gate-funded and zero-critical-incident.
